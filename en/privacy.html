Privacy

Privacy statement

This website collects only information that you choose to submit through the feedback and contact forms.

What information is collected?

Short feedback may include the language, section or chapter, rating, optional comment, page, version and submission time. A contact request may also include your name, email address, the text or parashah you are studying, what you have already tried, and your question.

How is the information used?

The information is used to improve the website and handbook, review your question and — when you provide contact details — possibly reply. It is not used for advertising or sold.

Processing via Web3Forms

The forms are technically processed via Web3Forms (web3forms.com) and forwarded as email to info@parasja.nl.

No sensitive information

Do not submit medical, legal, financial or other sensitive personal information. Use the contact form if you want a submission removed or have a question about it.
